You're scaling a platform sale into complex financial services accounts — banks, insurers, lenders — where the buyer is a CDO or CTO who's seen a hundred vendor decks. The challenge isn't awareness, it's conviction: turning a technically capable platform into a business case that survives CFO scrutiny and procurement cycles that run 6–12 months.

I've run exactly this playbook at FintechOS — prototype-led selling into UK and European financial services, with value cases up to €52M. I know what it takes to win enterprise fintech deals.
